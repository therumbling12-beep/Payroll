# Payroll Management

-   Add manual computation.
-   For example, if an employee was given the wrong salary, it should be
    possible to edit it.
-   The computation must be strictly based on Compensation Planning.
-   The salary computation must be accurate.
-   The payslip should show:
    -   Gross Pay
    -   Net Pay
    -   Deductions
    -   Incentives
    -   Claims & Reimbursement, if applicable
-   Compensation Planning and Payroll Management must perfectly match.
-   For 13th month pay:
    -   Before it is released, it must first be submitted to Admin for
        approval.
    -   Once Admin approves it, the system will request the budget from
        Financial.
    -   Once Financial provides the budget, Payroll can release the 13th
        month pay.
-   The mode of payment should also be included:
    -   Cash
    -   Bank
-   If the employee is paid through a bank, there should be a process
    for this.
    -   Example: BDO
    -   The employee must provide their bank details, such as their bank
        reference/account number.
    -   The salary will be assigned to that employee's bank information.
    -   Each employee should have their own bank information.
    -   There should also be a process for how the salary will be
        deposited into the employee's bank account.

# Compensation

-   For counter offers, there should also be a conversion/computation

# Claims and Reimbursement

    for a specific incentive, such as ₱500.

## Driver Incentives

-   Add subpages/navigation for driver incentives.
-   For example, if a driver qualifies for 20 rides and the incentive is
    ₱1,000, the amount and qualification should be shown in this module.
-   The system should determine whether the driver is qualified based on
    the configured incentive rules.

## Maternity Leave Request

-   There should also be a computation for this.

# HMO & Benefits

-   This module will handle the insurance and benefits of drivers,
    especially if they get into an accident and similar situations.
-   We need to ask the client what their current process is when a
    driver gets into an accident.
-   Questions to ask the client:
    -   Do they have a counter-offer or assistance process?
    -   Does the company pay the driver's medical or accident-related
        bills?
    -   Do they have an HMO specifically for drivers?
-   If the driver contributes to the benefit, for example, 3% of their
    salary is deducted, that deduction should go into their benefits.
