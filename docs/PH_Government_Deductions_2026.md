## 1. SSS – Social Security System

**Legal Basis:** Republic Act No. 11199 (Social Security Act of 2018) | SSS Circular No. 2024-06  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

### Contribution Rate Overview

| Party | Rate | Max Monthly Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee** | 5.0% of MSC | ₱1,750.00 |
| **Employer** | 10.0% of MSC | ₱3,500.00 |
| **Total** | **15.0% of MSC** | **₱5,250.00** |

> **Note:** Employer share also includes Employees' Compensation (EC) of ₱10 (MSC below ₱15,000) or ₱30 (MSC ₱15,000 and above).

### Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) Brackets

| MSC Range | Employee Share (5%) | Employer Share (10%) | EC | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₱5,000 (minimum) | ₱250.00 | ₱500.00 | ₱10.00 | ₱760.00 |
| ₱15,000 | ₱750.00 | ₱1,500.00 | ₱30.00 | ₱2,280.00 |
| ₱20,000 | ₱1,000.00 | ₱2,000.00 | ₱30.00 | ₱3,030.00 |
| ₱35,000 (maximum) | ₱1,750.00 | ₱3,500.00 | ₱30.00 | ₱5,280.00 |

### Key Details

- **Minimum MSC:** ₱5,000
- **Maximum MSC:** ₱35,000 (salary cap for computation)
- **Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF):** Applies to MSC above ₱20,000 — additional 2% Employee + 2% Employer on the excess
- **EC (Employees' Compensation):** Employer-only contribution; ₱10 for MSC < ₱15,000; ₱30 for MSC ≥ ₱15,000

### Computation Formula

```
Employee SSS Contribution = MSC × 5%
Employer SSS Contribution = MSC × 10% + EC
```

## 2. PhilHealth – Philippine Health Insurance Corporation

**Legal Basis:** Republic Act No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act) | PhilHealth Advisory No. 2025-0002  
**Effective Date:** January 2025

### Contribution Rate Overview

| Party | Rate | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employee** | 2.5% of Monthly Basic Salary | ₱250.00 | ₱2,500.00 |
| **Employer** | 2.5% of Monthly Basic Salary | ₱250.00 | ₱2,500.00 |
| **Total** | **5.0%** | **₱500.00** | **₱5,000.00** |

### Salary Bracket Examples

| Monthly Basic Salary | Total Premium (5%) | Employee Share (2.5%) | Employer Share (2.5%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₱10,000 (floor) | ₱500.00 | ₱250.00 | ₱250.00 |
| ₱20,000 | ₱1,000.00 | ₱500.00 | ₱500.00 |
| ₱50,000 | ₱2,500.00 | ₱1,250.00 | ₱1,250.00 |
| ₱100,000 (ceiling) | ₱5,000.00 | ₱2,500.00 | ₱2,500.00 |
| Above ₱100,000 | ₱5,000.00 | ₱2,500.00 | ₱2,500.00 |

### Key Details

- **Premium Rate:** 5.0% of Monthly Basic Salary (MBS)
- **Income Floor:** ₱10,000 — employees earning below this contribute based on ₱10,000
- **Income Ceiling:** ₱100,000 — contributions are capped regardless of salary above this
- **Employer & Employee Split:** 50/50 equal sharing
- **Kasambahay earning ≤ ₱5,000:** Employer pays the full ₱500 premium
- **Senior Citizens / Lifetime Members (120+ months):** Exempt from premium contributions

### Computation Formula

```
Total PhilHealth Premium = Monthly Basic Salary × 5%
Employee Share = Total Premium ÷ 2
Employer Share = Total Premium ÷ 2
```

## 3. Pag-IBIG – Home Development Mutual Fund (HDMF)

**Legal Basis:** Republic Act No. 9679 (HDMF Law of 2009) | HDMF Circular No. 460  
**Effective Date:** February 2024 (still in effect for 2025)

### Contribution Rate Overview

| Monthly Compensation | Employee Rate | Employer Rate | Total Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₱1,500 and below | 1% | 2% | 3% |
| Above ₱1,500 | **2%** | **2%** | **4%** |

### Maximum Fund Salary (MFS) Cap

| Compensation Level | Employee Share | Employer Share | Total Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to ₱10,000 | ₱200.00 (max) | ₱200.00 (max) | ₱400.00 |
| Above ₱10,000 | ₱200.00 (capped) | ₱200.00 (capped) | ₱400.00 |

> **Note:** The Maximum Fund Salary (MFS) was increased from ₱5,000 to ₱10,000 under HDMF Circular No. 460. Contributions are capped even if the employee earns more.

### Key Details

- **Maximum Fund Salary (MFS):** ₱10,000 (contributions computed up to this amount only)
- **Maximum Employee Contribution:** ₱200.00/month
- **Maximum Employer Contribution:** ₱200.00/month
- **Maximum Total Contribution:** ₱400.00/month
- **Voluntary Minimum:** ₱100.00/month (for OFWs, self-employed, voluntary members)
- **Kasambahay earning < ₱5,000:** Employer shoulders the full contribution

### Computation Formula

```
Pag-IBIG Contribution = MIN(Monthly Salary, ₱10,000) × 2%
Maximum Employee Deduction = ₱200.00/month
```

## 4. BIR – Bureau of Internal Revenue (Withholding Tax)

**Legal Basis:** TRAIN Law (Republic Act No. 10963) | BIR Revenue Regulations No. 13-2023 | RMC No. 05-2023  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2023 — ongoing (no changes for 2025)

### Annual Income Tax Table (TRAIN Law – Effective 2023 Onwards)

| Annual Taxable Income | Tax Due |
|---|---|
| ₱250,000 and below | **0% (Exempt)** |
| ₱250,001 – ₱400,000 | 15% of excess over ₱250,000 |
| ₱400,001 – ₱800,000 | ₱22,500 + 20% of excess over ₱400,000 |
| ₱800,001 – ₱2,000,000 | ₱102,500 + 25% of excess over ₱800,000 |
| ₱2,000,001 – ₱8,000,000 | ₱402,500 + 30% of excess over ₱2,000,000 |
| Over ₱8,000,000 | ₱2,202,500 + 35% of excess over ₱8,000,000 |

### Monthly Withholding Tax Table (for Employers)

| Monthly Taxable Compensation | Prescribed Tax Due | Plus (%) | On Excess Over |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₱0 – ₱20,833 | ₱0 | 0% | ₱0 |
| ₱20,834 – ₱33,332 | ₱0 | 15% | ₱20,833 |
| ₱33,333 – ₱66,666 | ₱1,875.00 | 20% | ₱33,333 |
| ₱66,667 – ₱166,666 | ₱8,541.80 | 25% | ₱66,667 |
| ₱166,667 – ₱666,666 | ₱33,541.80 | 30% | ₱166,667 |
| Over ₱666,667 | ₱183,541.80 | 35% | ₱666,667 |

### Key Details & Exemptions

- **Minimum Wage Earners:** Fully exempt from income tax, regardless of salary bracket
- **13th Month Pay & Other Benefits:** Exempt up to ₱90,000 annually; any excess is taxable
- **De Minimis Benefits:** Non-taxable up to BIR-prescribed limits per benefit type
- **Mandatory Contributions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG):** Deducted from gross income before computing taxable income
- **Withholding Tax Form:** BIR Form 1601-C (Monthly Remittance Return)
- **Remittance Deadline:** On or before the 10th of the following month (15th for eFPS/eBIRForms filers)
- **Year-End Document:** BIR Form 2316 (Certificate of Compensation Payment/Tax Withheld)

### Computation Formula

```
Taxable Income = Gross Salary − (SSS + PhilHealth + Pag-IBIG) − Non-taxable Benefits
Withholding Tax = Prescribed Tax Due + [(Taxable Income − Excess Over Amount) × Tax Rate %]
```

### Sample Computation

> **Employee:** Monthly salary of ₱50,000  
> **SSS Deduction:** ₱1,000 | **PhilHealth:** ₱625 | **Pag-IBIG:** ₱100  
> **Taxable Monthly Income:** ₱50,000 − ₱1,725 = ₱48,275  
> **Bracket:** ₱33,333 – ₱66,666  
> **Tax:** ₱1,875 + [(₱48,275 − ₱33,333) × 20%] = ₱1,875 + ₱2,988.40 = **₱4,863.40/month**

## 5. 13th Month Pay

**Legal Basis:** Presidential Decree No. 851 (13th Month Pay Law, 1975) | DOLE-BWC Handbook (p. 40–42) | DOLE Labor Advisory No. 06-20 | TRAIN Law (RA 10963) — Tax Exemption  
**Effective Date:** Ongoing — no changes for 2026

### What It Is

The 13th month pay is a **mandatory monetary benefit** for all rank-and-file employees in the private sector. It is **not** a bonus — it is required by law regardless of company size or profitability. The minimum amount is equivalent to one-twelfth (1/12) of an employee's total basic salary earned within the calendar year.

### Who Is Covered

| Employee Type | Entitled? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rank-and-file (regular, probationary, contractual, project-based) | ✅ Yes | Must have worked at least 1 month during the calendar year |
| Part-time employees | ✅ Yes | Pro-rated based on actual work |
| Resigned / separated employees | ✅ Yes | Pro-rated based on months served |
| Managerial employees | ❌ Not mandatory | Many companies extend it voluntarily; once established as practice, it becomes a contractual obligation |
| Government employees | ❌ Not covered by PD 851 | Covered under separate legislation (year-end bonus) |
| Kasambahay (household workers) | ✅ Yes | Computed on basic monthly salary |

### What Counts as Basic Salary

**Included:**
- Regular monthly basic salary
- COLA or allowances **only if integrated into basic salary** via employment contract, CBA, or established company practice

**Excluded:**
- Overtime pay
- Holiday pay and premium pay
- Night shift differential
- Cost of Living Allowances (COLA) — if paid separately
- Cash equivalent of unused vacation/sick leave credits
- 13th month pay itself, bonuses, and other non-regular benefits
- Commissions — unless treated as integrated basic salary per company policy or CBA

> **Source:** DOLE-BWC Handbook, p. 41; Presidential Decree No. 851

### Standard Computation Formula

```
13th Month Pay = Total Basic Salary Earned During the Calendar Year ÷ 12
```

**For full-year employees (same salary throughout):**
```
13th Month Pay = Monthly Basic Salary × 12 ÷ 12
             = Monthly Basic Salary (1 full month's pay)
```

**For mid-year hires, resigned, or employees with salary changes:**
```
13th Month Pay = (Sum of Monthly Basic Salaries Actually Earned Jan–Dec) ÷ 12
```

**Effect of unpaid absences:**
```
Adjusted Monthly Salary = Daily Rate × Number of Days Actually Paid
13th Month Pay = (Sum of All Adjusted Monthly Salaries) ÷ 12
Daily Rate (monthly-paid) = Monthly Basic Salary ÷ 26 (or as per company divisor)
```

### Sample Computations

**Example 1 — Full-Year Employee, Fixed Salary**
> Monthly basic salary: ₱35,000 | Worked all 12 months | No unpaid absences
```
₱35,000 × 12 = ₱420,000 total basic salary
₱420,000 ÷ 12 = ₱35,000 13th month pay
```

**Example 2 — Mid-Year Hire (hired July 1)**
> Monthly basic salary: ₱28,000 | 6 months worked (July–December)
```
₱28,000 × 6 = ₱168,000 total basic salary earned
₱168,000 ÷ 12 = ₱14,000 13th month pay
```

**Example 3 — Salary Change Mid-Year**
> Jan–Jun: ₱30,000/month | Jul–Dec: ₱38,000/month (after promotion)
```
(₱30,000 × 6) + (₱38,000 × 6) = ₱180,000 + ₱228,000 = ₱408,000
₱408,000 ÷ 12 = ₱34,000 13th month pay
```

**Example 4 — Employee with Unpaid Absences**
> Monthly basic salary: ₱40,000 | Daily rate: ₱40,000 ÷ 26 = ₱1,538.46  
> Took 10 days unpaid leave in March → March adjusted salary: 26−10 = 16 days paid
> March adjusted: 16 × ₱1,538.46 = ₱24,615.36 | All other months: ₱40,000 each
```
Total = (₱40,000 × 11) + ₱24,615.36 = ₱440,000 + ₱24,615.36 = ₱464,615.36
₱464,615.36 ÷ 12 = ₱38,717.95 13th month pay
```

### Tax Treatment (TRAIN Law)

| Component | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|
| 13th Month Pay + Other Bonuses (combined) up to ₱90,000 | **Tax-exempt** |
| Excess above ₱90,000 | Taxable as regular compensation income |

> **Note:** The ₱90,000 exemption cap is **calendar-year** based and applies to the combined total of 13th month pay, Christmas bonus, and other bonuses. Any taxable excess is included in the December withholding tax computation.

### Payment Deadlines

| Payment | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Full payment** | On or before **December 24** of the covered year |
| **1st installment (optional split)** | On or before **May 31** |
| **2nd installment (if split)** | On or before **December 24** |
| **Separated employees** | Within **30 days** from date of separation (per DOLE Labor Advisory No. 06-20) |
| **DOLE Compliance Report** | On or before **January 15** of the following year |

### Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to pay 13th month pay is a **labor law violation** and may result in:
- DOLE administrative fines and compliance orders
- Payment of overdue amounts with legal interest
- Possible work stoppage or business permit suspension
- Criminal prosecution under the Labor Code for willful non-payment

Employees may file a complaint at the nearest **DOLE Regional/Field Office**.

### Computation Formula (Quick Reference)

```
Full Year:      13th Month Pay = Monthly Basic Salary
Partial Year:   13th Month Pay = Total Basic Salary Earned ÷ 12
With Absences:  13th Month Pay = Sum of (Daily Rate × Paid Days per Month) ÷ 12
Tax-Exempt Up to: ₱90,000 combined (13th month + all other bonuses)
```
