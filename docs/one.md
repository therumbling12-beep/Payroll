# Payroll Management

- Payroll Management must have an accurate and automated salary computation.
- Payroll should be integrated with Workforce Management to get employee attendance.
- The system should automatically calculate the employee's salary based on the attendance data.
- Salary should not need to be manually calculated one employee at a time.
- The salary computation should depend on the employee's position.
- Payroll should automatically deduct:
  - SSS
  - PhilHealth
  - Pag-IBIG
- The system should identify how much is deducted for each contribution.
- The deductions are not the same for every employee because they depend on the employee's salary.
- There should be a payslip for every employee.
- The payslip should clearly show how much was deducted for:
  - SSS
  - PhilHealth
  - Pag-IBIG
- The deductions should be transparent to the employee.

# Compensation Planning

- Compensation Planning will determine how much an employee should be paid.
- Example:
  - Employee #1 has high credentials.
  - Employee #1 also has long work experience.
  - The system should determine how the company can properly compensate the employee based on these factors.
- Compensation Planning should be integrated with Applicant Management.
- Applicant Management will use Compensation Planning when making a counter offer to a good candidate.
- The counter offer should have a proper salary computation.
- Compensation Planning should determine salary ranges within the company.
- It should cover employees from the highest-paid position down to lower-paid positions, such as a janitor.
- The system should also determine how much an employee's salary can become based on their number of years of service.

# Claims & Reimbursement

- Claims & Reimbursement should be integrated with Fleet and Transportation Management.
- Drivers may have expenses that they need to be reimbursed for.
- Example:
  - Gas expenses
  - Other work-related driver expenses
- Once the expense has been approved and reimbursed, Fleet and Transportation Management should send the reimbursement information to Payroll.
- Payroll should include the reimbursement in the employee's pay.
- The reimbursement amount should also appear on the employee's payslip.

# HMO & Benefits

- HMO and Benefits will include benefits such as the 13th Month Pay and other company benefits.
- The HMO process will depend on the company.
- The client needs to be asked whether they have an existing HMO process.
- If the client has an HMO process, the system requirements should follow the client's actual process.

# HR Analytics Dashboard

- The HR Analytics Dashboard will mainly be used as a reporting dashboard.
- It should provide reports about the overall employee performance.
- It should also provide reports about the salaries paid by the company.
- The dashboard can show good and bad employee performance.
- The dashboard should provide an overview of employee performance and payroll-related information.
